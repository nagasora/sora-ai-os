# Research Experiment

1. Search existing research knowledge and prior decisions first.
2. Separate established fact, working hypothesis, and unresolved question.
3. State mathematical/theoretical assumptions explicitly.
4. Define experiment, controls, observables, expected outcomes, and falsification condition.
5. Record exact configuration and results.
6. Distinguish empirical observation from theoretical explanation.
7. Create a durable candidate only when the outcome or literature evidence can alter future reasoning.
